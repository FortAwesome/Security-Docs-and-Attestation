# SECURITY QUESTIONNAIRE RESPONSE POLICY

## Classification Level

Publicly available

## Review Information

__Mandatory Review Period__

Yearly

__Date of Last Review__

January 28, 2026

## Introduction

Vendor risk-management and security questionnaires are common in procurement workflows. To keep security information accurate, current, and consistently communicated, Fonticons, Inc. maintains a public set of security policies, attestations, and standard responses.

## Goal Statement

We need to establish guidelines to establish a fair, reasonable, and consistent means of addressing these common requests. While making it clear where the information is publicly available and when Fonticons, Inc. will and will not complete bespoke security questionnaires.

## Background Statement

The time required to fill out information that is publicly available is not commensurate with the cost of our service but we recognize that clients may have different security needs or requirements and want to be as helpful to our clients as possible.

## Definitions

### Terms

* The word "we" shall mean Fonticons, Inc., all Fonticons, Inc. employees and any individuals contracting with Fonticons, Inc. to complete work.
* The word Fonticons will be synonymous with Fonticons, Inc. for the purposes of this policy.
* Employee shall mean an individual directly employed by Fonticons, and all contractors, consultants, temporary employees, or business partners.
* Fonticons products/services refers to any and all paid or free products and/or services offered by Fonticons.
* Client shall mean a person or entity who installs or configures part or all of Fonticons product/service for use on a website or product not owned or otherwise controlled by Fonticons.
* Fonticons system shall mean any computers, communication systems, platforms, and any other information technology systems used by Fonticons, to provide the Font Awesome product and service.
* Font Awesome product shall mean the icons, their digital representation, and associated icon functionality present in code.
* Font Awesome service shall mean the technologies that make the Font Awesome product available to clients, users, and site users.
* Standard Account shall mean a self-serve, non-enterprise customer relationship without a negotiated security addendum and without a dedicated security review process.
* Annual Contract Value (ACV) shall mean the total contracted spend across Fonticons, Inc. products/services for a 12-month period, excluding taxes.

## Policy

1. For most security due-diligence needs, Fonticons, Inc. provides answers via our public security documentation. Customers and prospects should begin here:
   1. Request for Security Information (primary index): https://github.com/FortAwesome/Security-Docs-and-Attestation/blob/main/Request_for_Security_Information.md
   1. This documentation is intended to address common questionnaire topics (e.g., access controls, incident response, change management, data handling practices, and related security governance) without requiring a bespoke response.
1. To ensure consistent use of company resources, Fonticons, Inc. does not complete general security questionnaires for Standard Accounts with ACV below $4,000 USD per year.
   1. For these accounts, Fonticons, Inc. will instead:
      1. Direct requesters to the public security documentation repository (linked above), and
      1. Provide reasonable clarification limited to locating existing published materials (for example, pointing to the appropriate section/document), when practical.
1. For accounts with ACV at or above $4,000 USD per year, Fonticons, Inc. may consider completing a security questionnaire or participating in an enterprise due-diligence process, subject to:
   1. The nature and scope of the request;
   1. Reasonable time and effort requirements;
   1. Any applicable confidentiality requirements (e.g., NDA), if needed; and
   1. Fonticons, Inc.’s internal prioritization and security review procedures.
1. Some organizations require portal submissions or non-standard attachments. For Standard Accounts below the threshold, Fonticons, Inc. will not create or maintain portal-based responses and will instead rely on the published documentation as the authoritative source.

### Exceptions

Fonticons, Inc. may make exceptions at its sole discretion, including (but not limited to):
1. Regulatory or legal requirements that mandate a specific form of response;
1. Strategic partnerships;
1. Material changes in scope (e.g., elevated access, special integrations, or negotiated terms); or
1. Time-sensitive procurement needs where a minimal, targeted response is reasonable.
